How Accurate Is Our Automatic 360°-Camera Reconstruction Pipeline?

Benchmarking the OVR 360° Pipeline Against a Simultaneous LIDAR Survey of the Same Site

OVER Research

We rode a LIDAR scanner and our 360° camera on the same pole through a 40 × 25 m site and compared the two reconstructions point by point: median surface agreement of 3.9 cm — at the reference scanner's own declared accuracy — drift-free geometry over the 118 m path, and one uniform 2.9% scale disagreement that the measurement chain traces to the metric reference rather than the reconstruction, removable with a single scalar.

Can 1,000 Open Scenes Make a State-of-the-Art 3D Model Better?

Assessing the OVER Dataset by Fine-Tuning a 3D Foundation Model on Its Open Sample

OVER Research

An empirical assessment of the OVER dataset for research, run entirely on OverMaps-1k — the open 1,000-scene sample anyone can download from Hugging Face: pose accuracy improves visibly on our domain, catastrophic forgetting is measured and defused through WiSE weight merging, and a scaling analysis shows how much data the gain requires.
